Pixel Phone review

By Ana Lopez. Published March 3, 2026.

The Pixel Phone is a mid-range phone with a bright screen and a camera that punches above its price.

Screen

The screen is a 6.1 inch panel that stays readable in sunlight.

Battery life

The battery lasted a day and a half in our tests, and it charges to half in twenty minutes.

Price

It costs $499 unlocked, less with a carrier.
