Privacy Policy

Effective: March 17, 2026

GenEye AI Labs Inc. ("we", "us") builds MoltBrowser with privacy as a core architectural principle, not just a policy. This document explains what data MoltBrowser collects (almost nothing) and how it handles your information.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • AI conversations — All LLM inference runs 100% on your device. We never see your prompts, responses, or chat history.
  • Browsing history — We have no access to any pages you visit.
  • Telemetry — MoltBrowser sends no usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic data to our servers.
  • Keystrokes or form data — Nothing you type is transmitted to us.
  • Google services — MoltBrowser is de-Googled. No Google API keys, no Safe Browsing calls, no sync.

2. Data That Stays On Your Device

  • AI model files (downloaded from public model repositories)
  • Chat history and conversation exports
  • MoltBrowser settings and preferences
  • MoltShield filter lists (updated from public sources like EasyList)

3. Network Requests MoltBrowser Makes

MoltBrowser makes the following network requests, all to public infrastructure:

  • Model downloads — When you choose to download an AI model, MoltBrowser fetches it from Hugging Face or the model's public CDN.
  • Auto-update checks — MoltBrowser periodically checks moltbrowser.com/updates/appcast.xml for new versions. This request contains only your current version number and OS. No personally identifiable information is sent.
  • Filter list updates — MoltShield downloads public ad-blocking filter lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy) from their official sources.
  • Chrome extensions — If you install extensions, they may make their own network requests per their own privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Extensions

Chrome extensions you install may collect data according to their own privacy policies. MoltBrowser does not control third-party extension behavior. We recommend reviewing extension permissions before installing.

5. Children's Privacy

MoltBrowser does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if MoltBrowser adds features that affect data handling. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at privacy@geneye.ai.
