Trams return to Lyon after ten years

The city opens a new tram line this weekend, ten years after the last one closed.

Transport

The new line runs from the station to the university every six minutes.

What riders say

Riders say the trams are quiet and the stops are close together.

Timetable

The first tram leaves at five in the morning and the last one at midnight.
